## HOW TO READ THIS

Nobody scrolls through their own life thinking I have poor boundaries.

They think: I leaned on the horn at a stranger this morning. I cancelled on my family again. I redid the apprentice's work at 9pm.

That is where this starts. The moment, not the diagnosis.

Sixteen pages follow. Each one opens on something small and specific that most people have done and almost nobody has said out loud. Read the moment first. If it is not yours, turn the page. There is no prize for collecting these.

If it is yours, keep reading down the page. The name sits at the bottom, and it is deliberately at the bottom. A name handed to you at the top is a label. A name you arrive at yourself is a recognition. Only one of those changes anything.

Every one of the sixteen runs on the same loop. A belief drives a thought. The thought drives what you feel. What you feel drives what you do. What you do produces a result. And then the result walks back around and hands the belief its evidence.

That is why discipline keeps failing you. Discipline attacks the doing. The belief underneath just keeps manufacturing more of the same.

None of these are flaws. Every single one is protection that outlived the season it was built for. We honour what kept you safe. Now we build what lets you breathe.

## THE SIXTEEN

### 01 · BURNOUT

**The moment.** The driver at the lights did nothing. They were slow off the green and you leaned on the horn like they had cost you the whole day. It was 7:45am.

**You have been calling it.** Stress. A big season. Just life.

**What it actually is.** This is not tiredness. Tiredness announces itself and asks to lie down. This does not. This leaks out sideways, onto whoever is closest and least deserving of it.

**The loop.** It all falls apart without me, so just get through this week, so everything is a spark near a short fuse, so it comes out at the apprentice and the kids and a stranger at the lights. And now it really is all on you, because who else were you ever going to ask.

**And this is what it is called.** BURNOUT

**The question.** Who got the version of you that you would never have chosen to give them?

### 02 · THE PLEASER

**The moment.** You said yes to the extra swing before you told anyone at home. You worked out how to explain it on the drive.

**You have been calling it.** Loyalty. Reliability. Providing. Doing the right thing by everyone.

**What it actually is.** The yes is not generosity. It is the price of being needed, and being needed has stood in for being valued so long that you can no longer tell the two apart.

**The loop.** I matter when I am needed, so if I say no they will manage without me and then what am I, so guilt arrives the second a no even forms, so yes to the boss and the swing and the stay back and the cancelled holiday. Everyone learns your yes is free, the people at home get whatever is left, and being needed keeps doing the job that being loved was meant to do.

**And this is what it is called.** THE PLEASER

**The question.** What did you say yes to this month that you had already decided against?

---

### 03 · THE SELF LENS

**The moment.** Nobody has brought you a problem in months. They bring you fires, already burning, already too late to be small.

**You have been calling it.** High standards. Excellence. Strong leadership.

**What it actually is.** The standards are not the problem. Standards held in integrity with your own values build people. These ones measure them. And a person who feels measured will hide the exact thing you most needed to see.

**The loop.** My value is my performance and so is everyone else's, so why can they not keep up, so contempt arrives wearing frustration's coat, so they get a verdict instead of feedback and a bar nobody clears. You end up with a room that performs for you and hides from you, respect for the title and not the person, and more proving to fill the gap the proving opened.

**And this is what it is called.** THE SELF LENS

**The question.** When did someone last tell you an uncomfortable truth to your face?

### 04 · COMMUNICATION

**The moment.** The fight was not about the dishwasher. You both knew that by the second sentence and neither of you said so.

**You have been calling it.** Keeping the peace. Not sweating the small stuff. Being easy to live with.

**What it actually is.** You ran three weeks of fine and then detonated over a wet towel. The towel was never carrying the charge. The charge is a sentence you have not said since March.

**The loop.** If I say what I actually need it will cost me, so it is not worth the fight, so resentment goes quiet and a very specific loneliness sets in, the kind that only happens inside a relationship, so you hint, and joke with an edge, and say it's fine, and then the towel. They still do not know what you need, so you decide they do not care, and the belief gets handed its evidence.

**And this is what it is called.** COMMUNICATION

**The question.** What is the sentence, and who is it for?

### 05 · THE URGENCY ADDICT

**The moment.** You checked your email before your feet touched the floor. 5:30am, in the dark, and something in you settled once you had.

**You have been calling it.** Responsive. Across it. Switched on.

**What it actually is.** Calm reads as boredom to a system that has run on alarm this long. So when nothing is on fire, something quietly gets lit. Not on purpose. Just reliably.

**The loop.** If I am not on it, it burns down, so I will just check, so there is a low hum of alarm that has started to feel like being alive, so the phone comes to dinner and the laptop comes on the holiday and you are never once unreachable. You get a life with no air in it, and every fire heroically put out proves the whole thing over again.

**And this is what it is called.** THE URGENCY ADDICT

**The question.** When were you last genuinely unreachable, and what did you do with the hour?

### 06 · THE MARTYR

**The moment.** Three people offered to help and you said no to all of them. Then you did it alone, and by Sunday night you could not look at any of them.

**You have been calling it.** Selflessness. The backbone. Holding it all together.

**What it actually is.** The giving has an invoice attached and nobody else has seen it. There is a ledger running in you that the people you love have never been shown, and are somehow still in debt on.

**The loop.** Love and respect are earned by sacrifice, so nobody else will do it properly, fine, I will, so resentment builds with no voice and nowhere to go, so you do all of it and refuse every offer and bill them emotionally three weeks later. Then the explosion over nothing, then the guilt, and a family who cannot settle an account they were never handed.

**And this is what it is called.** THE MARTYR

**The question.** What are you keeping score of, and who would be shocked to learn the score?

### 07 · THE CONTROLLER

**The moment.** You redid the apprentice's work at 9pm and told yourself it was faster this way. You have been telling yourself that for two years.

**You have been calling it.** High standards. Efficiency. If you want it done right.

**What it actually is.** Underneath the distrust is a quieter question you have never said out loud. If someone else can do this properly, what exactly am I.

**The loop.** Nobody does it right and my worth is in the doing, so it is faster to do it myself, so there is tension every time it leaves my hands, so you redo finished work and repack a packed bag and take over the school project. You become the bottleneck, the team learns to wait for your recheck, and now it is genuinely true that nobody else can do it.

**And this is what it is called.** THE CONTROLLER

**The question.** What are you still doing at 9pm that someone else was already doing at 3?

### 08 · THE PEACEKEEPER

**The moment.** It's fine, you said. Full sentence. Three weeks running.

**You have been calling it.** Easygoing. Low maintenance. Not a drama person.

**What it actually is.** The unsaid does not evaporate. It stacks up like freight in a yard nobody is unloading, and freight that heavy always comes off the rails somewhere small.

**The loop.** Honesty costs the relationship, so let it go, it is not worth it, so the frustration gets swallowed and the gap widens, so it's fine and whatever you want, and then the detonation. The real thing never gets addressed, the explosion proves that speaking up makes it worse, and back to fine you go.

**And this is what it is called.** THE PEACEKEEPER

**The question.** What have you let go of so many times that it has quietly become the thing?

### 09 · THE PROVER

**The moment.** You got the thing you had been working toward for five years. By Tuesday you were flat, and slightly embarrassed about being flat.

**You have been calling it.** Ambition. Drive. Hunger.

**What it actually is.** The goalpost moves on contact. It has to. Because if a win were ever allowed to land, you would have to sit with who you are when you are not producing, and that is a room you have never once walked into.

**The loop.** I am what I achieve and I will be enough when, so I will rest when I get there, so there is a short hit at the win and then nothing, so the celebration gets skipped and the next thing starts before the first one is cold. You end up with a highlight reel that feels like static, people who love you celebrating on their own, and enough never once arriving.

**And this is what it is called.** THE PROVER

**The question.** Name the last thing you achieved. Now name what you did the next morning.

### 10 · THE PERFECTIONIST STALL

**The moment.** The quote is written. It has been written for eleven days. It is sitting in drafts while you work out how to word one line.

**You have been calling it.** Doing it properly. High standards. Attention to detail.

**What it actually is.** This is hiding, wearing the most respectable outfit hiding has ever owned. Nothing that stays in drafts can be judged. Nothing that stays in drafts can pay you either.

**The loop.** If it is not perfect I will be seen failing, so it is not ready yet, so there is dread about being watched mid-process, so you write the fourth version and move the date and never send the quote. The thing never ships, the opportunity expires in the polishing, and because nothing was ever risked the belief stays untested and stays in charge.

**And this is what it is called.** THE PERFECTIONIST STALL

**The question.** What is finished, and still not sent?

### 11 · THE NUMB-OUT

**The moment.** 10:47pm. Two hours into the phone. You could not tell anyone what you looked at, and the alarm is set for 4:45.

**You have been calling it.** Unwinding. Me time. I have earned this.

**What it actually is.** This is the only hour of the day when nobody is allowed to need you, and it is being spent going numb rather than being spent resting. Numbing pauses the depletion. It has never once refilled anything.

**The loop.** This is the only relief on offer, so I have earned this, so depletion turns up in relaxation's costume, so there is the scroll and the third glass on a Tuesday and the rubbish on the screen past midnight. You wake more tired than you went to bed, and you quietly conclude that this hour really is the best the day has in it.

**And this is what it is called.** THE NUMB-OUT

**The question.** What would actually restore you, if that hour were spent on purpose?

### 12 · THE ESCAPE FANTASY

**The moment.** You googled cabins on your lunch break again. You have never once looked at the price seriously.

**You have been calling it.** Daydreaming. Wouldn't it be nice.

**What it actually is.** This is the most honest thing your system does all day. It is a vote, and it is voting against the design, not against the life. You do not want to escape your life. You want a life that does not need escaping, and those are two completely different projects. Only one of them requires burning anything down.

**The loop.** This is what life costs and the real thing is out of reach for people like me, so one day we will sell it all, so the longing builds with nowhere to put itself, so there are cabins at lunch and that feeling driving past the airport and the half worked out lotto plan. The fantasy gets fed, the design never changes, and that proves the real thing was out of reach all along.

**Lived proof.** We did not escape ours. We redesigned it, and the boat we live on is the evidence.

**And this is what it is called.** THE ESCAPE FANTASY

**The question.** What are you trying to escape, and what would you redesign instead?

### 13 · THE BUSY ADDICT

**The moment.** Day two of the holiday and you were itchy. Then you started reorganising the pantry while your kid was mid-sentence.

**You have been calling it.** Productivity. Work ethic. A full life.

**What it actually is.** Stillness is where the feelings are kept, so the calendar stays full as a form of protection. It is very effective protection. It also means nothing gets to land, and nothing gets to mend.

**The loop.** Empty time is wasted time and stillness is not safe, so I will just get one more thing done, so restlessness arrives the instant nothing is required, so every gap gets filled and the phone comes to the film and you work through the break. There is no space left for anything to arrive or repair, and stillness gets more frightening the longer it is avoided.

**And this is what it is called.** THE BUSY ADDICT

**The question.** What comes up in the first ten minutes of nothing?

### 14 · THE ARMOUR

**The moment.** I'm fine. You have said it to everyone and meant it for no one. Someone who loves you has told you that you never let them in, and you did not have an answer.

**You have been calling it.** Strength. Independence. Low maintenance.

**What it actually is.** You were trained in this. Harden up, push through, do not bring it home. It was good training for the world you were in, and it worked, and it protected you. Then it kept working long after the danger passed, and the thing that protected you started doing the isolating.

**The loop.** Needing is weakness, and if they saw the real thing they would leave or lose respect, so I will handle it myself, so there is loneliness inside a full house, so you never ask and never receive and deflect every are you okay. Nobody knows you, connection starves on what it is given, and the isolation becomes the proof that nobody can be let in.

**And this is what it is called.** THE ARMOUR

**The question.** You are better at asking are you okay than answering it. Who would you answer honestly, if you decided to?

### 15 · THE 3AM BOARD MEETING

**The moment.** 3:14am. Wide awake, winning an argument with someone who is asleep in the next room.

**You have been calling it.** Thorough. Switched on. Just a busy mind.

**What it actually is.** Vigilance became the job so long ago that the mind lost its off switch. Somewhere in there is a belief that something bad happens the moment you stop watching, and it has never been tested, because you have never stopped.

**The loop.** If I stop scanning I will get caught out, so let me run it once more, so there is wired tiredness and alarm with no event attached to it, so you replay the meeting and rehearse the conversation and run tomorrow before it arrives. The mornings are exhausted, the conversations still never happen out loud, and the exhaustion creates the chaos that funds the vigilance all over again.

**And this is what it is called.** THE 3AM BOARD MEETING

**The question.** What conversation are you rehearsing at 3am instead of having at 3pm?

### 16 · THE FLATLINE

**The moment.** You smiled for the photo. You felt nothing. And then you felt a small, cold panic about feeling nothing.

**You have been calling it.** Staying humble. Hard to impress. Just not a celebrator.

**What it actually is.** This is where the chase ends if nothing gets rewired. The tank ran dry so long ago that even the good things cannot land now, and the numbness is being read as evidence that you need to push harder.

**The loop.** Feeling it is not safe, or not the point, keep moving, so on to the next, so there is nothing exactly where something should be, so the congratulations get deflected and you are back at the desk in the morning. You get a life that photographs beautifully and feels like static, and the flatness gets taken as proof you have not done enough yet.

**And this is what it is called.** THE FLATLINE

**The question.** When did something last actually land?
